Safety & guidance

Green means no configured threshold was crossed, not that pregnancy complications are ruled out. Rest for five minutes before measuring, seated with arm supported. Follow your maternity team's individual plan.

140 systolic OR 90 diastolic or higher: contact your maternity team promptly today. 160 systolic OR 110 diastolic or higher: seek urgent maternity assessment immediately; do not wait for the app or another reading.

Severe headache, visual changes, upper abdominal pain, chest pain, fainting or significant breathlessness require urgent medical attention regardless of readings. Call 112 for emergencies in the EU.

Heart rate has no universal normal upper limit in pregnancy. Pulse flags are conservative tracking prompts, not diagnostic criteria; persistent fast resting pulse needs clinical review even without symptoms. Low BP or pulse flags also depend on symptoms and baseline.
